Analyze a photo

Upload one full-body photo of a single person, standing and facing the camera with arms slightly away from the body. PhyVision classifies the physique and shows a matching fitness guide.

  • Portrait photo, about 2:3 or 3:4
  • One person, head to feet in frame
  • Body fills 75-95% of the height
  • Facing the camera, arms slightly out
  • Fitted clothes, plain background
  • Full photo guide
Uploads need the PhyVision server. In the project folder run python app.py, then open the address it prints. The demo people below work offline.
