Take a photo PhyVision can read

PhyVision classifies the outline of your body. The closer your photo is to the one in the diagram, the cleaner that outline and the more reliable the result.

In one sentence: a portrait photo of one person standing straight and facing the camera, head to feet in frame, arms slightly away from the body, in fitted clothes, against a plain background.

How much of the body to include

PhyVision estimates how much of your body is in the photo and adjusts for it. Head to feet is still the most reliable. Accuracy figures are from PhyVision's held-out test photos, with the person cut out automatically.

Pose, clothing, background and light

Pose

  • Stand straight, facing the camera.
  • Feet about hip-width apart.
  • Arms relaxed and 10 to 20 cm away from your sides, so the gap between arm and waist is visible.
  • Also supported: hands on hips, hand in pocket, a bicep flex, one arm raised.

Clothing

  • Fitted clothes: sportswear, a T-shirt, leggings or shorts. Shirtless is fine.
  • Avoid coats, hoodies, loose dresses, bags and anything that hides your outline.
  • Clothes that contrast with the background help the cut-out.

Background

  • A plain wall or door, ideally light and evenly coloured.
  • No other people, mirrors, pets or furniture touching you.
  • Stand a step away from the wall to avoid a hard shadow.

Light and editing

  • Even light from the front, such as facing a window.
  • Avoid standing in front of a bright window (backlit).
  • No filters, body-shaping edits, stickers or text over the body.

Quick checklist

Do

  • Portrait photo, about 2:3 or 3:4
  • One person, whole body, head to feet
  • Facing the camera, standing straight
  • Arms slightly away from the body
  • Fitted clothes, plain background, even light
  • Camera 2 to 3 m away at waist height

Avoid

  • Landscape or square photos
  • Group photos or people in the background
  • Side, back, sitting, lying or bending poses
  • Arms crossed or held tight against the body
  • Baggy clothing, coats, bags
  • Close-up and mirror selfies, filters

What PhyVision checks for you

Every result includes a photo check. Green items match the guide, amber items lower confidence a little, and red items make the result unreliable.

Resolution

At least 400 px tall.

Orientation

Portrait, width:height below 0.95.

One person

Only one body-sized shape is found.

Background

The person was separated from the background.

Person size

The body fills at least 70% of the photo height.

Head in frame

The head is not cut off at the top.

Centred

The body is near the middle and not touching the sides.

Framing

The model's estimate of how much of the body is visible.

Privacy

When you run PhyVision yourself, photos are processed on your own computer by the local server and are not uploaded anywhere else. Only analyze photos of yourself or of people who have agreed.
